Having trouble with the app, your subscription, or workout data? This page explains how to get help quickly and how to contact us.

🆘 Need Help Quickly?

If you are experiencing issues or have questions, you may contact support directly:

📩 fitMLapp@yahoo.com

🔧 Common Topics & Troubleshooting

Here are some of the most common areas we can help with:

Topic How We Help
Subscription & Billing Issues unlocking premium features, restoring purchases, or subscription questions.
Accounts & Login Apple/Email sign-in issues, verification problems, or login errors.
Workout Tracking Missing sets, incorrect stats, editing or deleting workouts, or exercise data issues.
Backups & Data Cloud backup/restore help, switching devices, or keeping history in sync.
App Bugs & Crashes Broken screens, errors, crashes, visual glitches, or unexpected behavior.
Feature Requests Suggest new metrics, workout templates, graphs, or analytics you’d like to see.

If your question isn’t listed here, please email us — we’re happy to help.

📝 How to Report an Issue

Including a few details in your report helps us resolve issues much faster:

  • Device model (e.g., iPhone 12, Pixel 8, etc.)
  • OS version
  • Step-by-step description of what you were doing
  • Screenshots or a short screen recording, if possible

💬 Feature Requests & Feedback

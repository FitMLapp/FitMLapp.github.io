# FitML — Smarter Workout Tracking

FitML is an intelligent fitness tracker built for people who want **better insights**, **cleaner tracking**, and **real progress** — without the clutter.

**Track workouts. Hit goals. See what’s improving.**

---

## What FitML Helps You Do

### ✅ Track everything (fast)
Log strength workouts, sets, reps, weight, and workout history with a clean workflow built for consistency.

### 📈 See progress clearly
View performance trends, recent bests, and exercise history so you always know what’s moving up.

### 🎯 Set goals and stay on pace
Create goals and track when you hit them — no spreadsheets needed.

### 🧠 Generate routines & analyze them (Premium)
FitML can generate routines and provide routine analysis to help you train smarter with structure.

### 🏋️ Share your results
Create share cards for:
- Workouts
- New records
- Goal hits
- Exercises

---

## Who FitML Is For

- Lifters who want **real stats** (not just checkmarks)
- People training for strength, hypertrophy, or consistency
- Anyone who wants a tracker that feels modern and data-driven
